## Summary

**Root cause:** The `.brand h1` element in the topbar was still visible on mobile — it was being replaced with the text "Dockyard" via a `::after` pseudo-element rather than being hidden. On small screens (≤640px and ≤480px), the title text takes up unnecessary horizontal space in the already-crowded topbar, especially since the bottom tab nav already provides navigation.

**What I changed in `web/src/styles.css`:**

1. **≤640px breakpoint (line 3351-3356):** Replaced the `font-size: 0` + `::after` hack (which showed "Dockyard") with `display: none` on `.brand h1`. The brand mark (◈ icon) stays visible as a compact home button.

2. **≤480px breakpoint (line 3661-3666):** Same change — replaced the `font-size: 0` + `::after` pattern with `display: none`.

The brand mark (◈) is preserved in both breakpoints as a minimal clickable home link, while the title text ("dockyard.ai") is fully hidden.
